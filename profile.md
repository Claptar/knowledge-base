## How I study

- Understanding means being able to answer "how could I come up with this?" — for the question,
  the definition, and the proof technique.
- The definition-then-theorem-then-convenient-proof style feels artificial; I want the motivation
  that forces the definition.
- Proofs are mine to do. A handed-over proof removes the point.
- Connections between topics matter more than coverage of any one of them.
- No introductory material — substantive sources only.
- Default session mode: Socratic, but ask me each time.
